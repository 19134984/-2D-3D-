                                                                                                                  What are further implications of our findings? First, it
                                                                                                               offers a further prominent example of nonlinear multiscale
                                                                                                               dynamics for which a characteristic local structural unit, or
                                                                                                               the building block, grows successively to the largest scales of
                                                                                                               the system in a hierarchical manner. Our findings indicate
                                                                                                               that, though the phenomena are complex, they are seated in
                                                                                                               the instabilities in the boundary layer, and are predominantly
                                                                                                               local in character. They then follow a dynamical process of
                                                                                                               coagulation or aggregation, which we have successfully modeled
                                                                                                               by the Smoluchowski equation. Our analysis underlines the
                                                                                                               likelihood that convective turbulence in this configuration
                                                                                                               follows a bottom–up scenario. Second, the essential agree-
                                                                                                               ment between the local instability calculations and simulations
                                                                                                               suggests that a global instability mechanism in the boundary
                                                                                                               layers is unlikely, at least for the parameter range accessed
                                                                                                               in the present simulations. Finally, we note that efforts are
                                                                                                               currently under way to extend the range of Rayleigh number
                                                                                                               to higher values for this configuration, as was done in ref.
                                                                                                               75 for a slender closed cell. Those results will be reported
                                                                                                               elsewhere.
