                                                                                        4. GPU implementation

                                                                                            The present GPU implementation is an extension of Chang et al.
                                                                                        [25] and Hong et al. [26]. The ﬂowchart of the GPU implementation
                                                                                        is shown in Fig. 3. All the variables, such as distribution function
                                                                                        and macroscopic quantities, are declared and initialized in host and
                                                                                        device before being executed on the kernel. After initialization, the
                                                                                        data are copied from host memory and to the device global mem-
                                                                                        ory and then executed on the kernel. The data transfer between
                                                                                        the host and device is conducted in the beginning and at the end
                                                                                        of the kernel. To avoid synchronization problems, two density dis-
                                                                                        tribution function arrays are created, one for reading and the other
                                                                                        one for storing.
                                                                                            The present multi-GPU implementation is built on the MPI en-
                                                                                        vironment. To attain higher scalability, the strategy proposed by
                                                                                        Wang and Aoki [27] is adopted in the present work, where the
                                                                                        computation is overlapped with communication, i.e. to hide the
                                                                                        latency of communication. Parallel computation is based on one-
                                                                                        dimensional domain decomposition. The relative performances of
                                                                                        GPU over CPU were addressed in Hong et al. [26,28].

                                                                                       6. Conclusions

                                                                                          Direct numerical simulations of wall-bounded turbulent chan-
                                                                                       nel ﬂow up to Reτ =640 with a grid density of ∼ 360 × 106
                                                                                       are simulated with multi-relaxation-time (MRT) lattice Boltzmann
                                                                                       models, D3Q19 and D3Q27. In order to resolve the wall bound-
                                                                                       ary layer, a successive reﬁnement procedure is developed to cap-
                                                                                       ture the rapid variation of the ﬂow properties in the near-wall
                                                                                       region. The interaction between different grid levels is through
                                                                                       the post-collision distribution function by ensuring the equality of
                                                                                       the stresses. For data transfer from coarse to ﬁne grids, the miss-
                                                                                       ing population is obtained by interpolation through a cubic poly-
                                                                                       nomial. In the present implementation, three levels of grids are
                                                                                       adopted with grid spacings in wall unit being 1 < + < 4. Due to
                                                                                       the explicit nature of the numerical scheme, the numerical proce-
                                                                                       dure is built on the CUDA framework and the simulations are con-
                                                                                       ducted on the GPU cluster. Strong scaling shows good scalability of
                                                                                       the present numerical procedure. The predicted mean streamwise
                                                                                       velocity compares favorably with the benchmark solutions, where
                                                                                       the present grid reﬁnement procedure well resolves the viscous
                                                                                       sub-layer. The turbulence anisotropy of the Reynolds stresses is
                                                                                       also correctly predicted. Turbulence transport in the channel is fur-
