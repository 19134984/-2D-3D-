              Abstract. Recent experimental, numerical and theoretical advances in turbulent Rayleigh-Bénard convec-
              tion are presented. Particular emphasis is given to the physics and structure of the thermal and velocity
              boundary layers which play a key role for the better understanding of the turbulent transport of heat and
              momentum in convection at high and very high Rayleigh numbers. We also discuss important extensions of
              Rayleigh-Bénard convection such as non-Oberbeck-Boussinesq eﬀects and convection with phase changes.
